About this report

This report explores how the mass timber industry’s products and real estate projects can better integrate into mainstream capital market flows. It records what institutional investors and developers ask when confronted by a mass timber proposal. Rather than interpretation, the content is the distilled answers of fund managers, investment officers and developers who oversee billions at globally recognized investment firms. From this output, the factors driving investment and development decisions are organized to frame and facilitate discussion.

Professionals with direct experience in institutional capital flows were consulted from 2024 to 2026 — pensions and insurers, allocators and funds, and developers — on the premise that the questions prudent fiduciaries ask should set the forward agenda for the industry, university and policy professionals working to hasten and scale adoption. The 33 questions produced sort into four families: physical (7), economic (17), regulatory (5) and execution (4). The physical questions have substantial, reliable answers in technical guides, codes and test programs; the economic questions — cost volatility, rent and retention, operating expense, cap and discount rates, the value of schedule — are seen today as mostly anecdote-based. Responses are consolidated and non-attributable so that participants remain anonymous.
