About Us

A platform for finance and consumer considerations

Mission

Advancing mass timber's financial, consumer and entrepreneurial foundations by curating applicable research into practice and connecting thought leadership across academia, industry and capital.

Request for Comment

Progress is built on the thoughtful exchange of ideas and respect for the work of others. MTFC is committed to honoring the intellectual property and contributions of creators — researchers and practitioners alike. Questions or concerns? We warmly invite you to contact us.
