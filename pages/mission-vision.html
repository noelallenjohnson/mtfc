Why the working group exists

Hypothesis

Mass timber needs more finance and consumer acumen

Demand-side factors are under-investigated. Supply-side considerations — from design and engineering to manufacturing, construction and regulatory headwinds — are no longer the primary hold-up for mass timber's growth. Western capital markets are deep, sophisticated and open to mass timber-related investment; they are neither for nor against it. What is missing is applied financial and consumer insight. Within the mass timber ecosystem, this acumen is often absent, secondary, or presented through under-informed analogy.

Because financial and consumer concepts feel familiar from everyday life, they're easy to misapply. The result is delayed capital formation: developers lack clear consumer value-creation frameworks, investors struggle to underwrite risk and return, research from unrelated disciplines confuses, and policy is driven by normative rather than objective heuristics. For any mass timber value proposition, the outstanding question is how it maps to a financial model: which assumption is adjusted, and by how much?

Response

A credible demand-side platform can help

MTFC provides the impartial platform and working group needed to align academic-quality insight with institutional investment requirements and real estate development realities. It operates at the intersection of academic research, institutional investment and real estate development as they bear on the mass timber ecosystem, and it convenes safe-harbor working groups to define, support and disseminate the applied research that is needed.
