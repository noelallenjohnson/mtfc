Resilience Value Adjustment Tool

Status: Underway

Author(s): Dr. Shiling Pei, Noel Johnson
Sponsor(s): Pending funding awards

Abstract

An underwriting tool that helps financial professionals adjust discount rates for location-specific risk and building-specific resilience, so that above-code design and performance – like that enabled by CLT rocking shear walls – can be valued and underwritten. Co-led by Dr. Shiling Pei and Noel Johnson.

Technical paper submitted.
