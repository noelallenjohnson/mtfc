Mass Timber Collaboration
Finance & Consumer
Research to Practice

The Mass Timber Finance & Consumer (MTFC) working group exists to advance our understanding of the consumer preferences, finance and entrepreneurial forces shaping the mass timber ecosystem.

MTFC's identity is educational and catalytic, not advocacy. Its aim is to foster and curate insights useful to investment, real estate development, marketing, design, engineering and government players.

Hypothesis

Mass timber needs more finance and consumer acumen

Supply-side hurdles are no longer the main hold-up. Capital markets are open to mass timber, neither for nor against it. What is missing is applied financial and consumer insight: for any claimed benefit, which proforma assumption changes, and by how much?

Response

A credible demand-side platform can help

MTFC is an impartial working group that aligns academic-quality insight with what institutional investors and developers need. Read the fuller discussion →

Objectives

01

Advance Applied, Useful Research

Foster consumer and finance driven research on capital structuring, risk-adjusted returns, underwriting standards, and cost-of-capital dynamics.

02

Build Financial Fluency of Firms & Projects

Translate capital market flows and access concepts to help practitioners build economically sustainable firms and projects.

03

Engage Institutional Capital

Create a credible interface between business schools, entrepreneurship centers, finance and marketing departments and large asset management firms/practitioners to facilitate informed diligence for capital allocation.

04

Serve as a Global Academic Node

Enable cross-university research, support grant-funded projects, and foster collaboration among proven experts in finance, consumer behavior, development, industry and product structure.
