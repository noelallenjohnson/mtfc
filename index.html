Mass Timber Collaboration
Finance & Consumer
Research to Practice

The Mass Timber Finance & Consumer (MTFC) working group exists to advance our understanding of the consumer preferences, finance and entrepreneurial forces shaping the mass timber ecosystem.

MTFC's identity is educational and catalytic, not advocacy. Its aim is to foster and curate insights useful to investment, real estate development, marketing, design, engineering and government players.

Hypothesis

Mass timber needs more finance and consumer acumen

Demand-side factors are under-investigated. Supply-side considerations — from design and engineering to manufacturing, construction and regulatory headwinds — are no longer the primary hold-up for mass timber's growth. Western capital markets are deep, sophisticated and open to mass timber-related investment; they are neither for nor against it. What is missing is applied financial and consumer insight. Within the mass timber ecosystem, this acumen is often absent, secondary, or presented through under-informed analogy.

Because financial and consumer concepts feel familiar from everyday life, they're easy to misapply. The result is delayed capital formation: developers lack clear consumer value-creation frameworks, investors struggle to underwrite risk and return, research from unrelated disciplines confuses, and policy is driven by normative rather than objective heuristics. For any mass timber value proposition, the outstanding question is how it maps to a financial model: which assumption is adjusted, and by how much?

Response

A credible demand-side platform can help

MTFC provides the impartial platform and working group needed to align academic-quality insight with institutional investment requirements and real estate development realities. It operates at the intersection of academic research, institutional investment and real estate development as they bear on the mass timber ecosystem, and it convenes safe-harbor working groups to define, support and disseminate the applied research that is needed.

Objectives

01

Advance Applied, Useful Research

Foster consumer and finance driven research on capital structuring, risk-adjusted returns, underwriting standards, and cost-of-capital dynamics.

02

Build Financial Fluency of Firms & Projects

Translate capital market flows and access concepts to help practitioners build economically sustainable firms and projects.

03

Engage Institutional Capital

Create a credible interface between business schools, entrepreneurship centers, finance and marketing departments and large asset management firms/practitioners to facilitate informed diligence for capital allocation.

04

Serve as a Global Academic Node

Enable cross-university research, support grant-funded projects, and foster collaboration among proven experts in finance, consumer behavior, development, industry and product structure.

Finance. Consumers. Timber's Future.

Insights — from Academy and Practice

Research projects, practitioner engagements, and market rate development demonstrations across the demand side, supply side, and capital markets of the mass timber economy.

Demand-Side · Consumer Focused

Underwriting Mass Timber Apartments

A revealed-preference survey of renters living in mass timber apartments, examining who values mass timber and how that value shows up in the proforma, the product and the project.

Status: Working paper posted (Nov 2026)

Industry Perceptions: Value Creation Mechanisms

An assessment of supply-side industry perceptions of how and where mass timber products create value, and of where the ecosystem's perceptions go wrong.

Status: Feasibility Complete

Mass Timber Economic Taxonomy

A proposed standard framework defining the means of value creation across mass timber products and buildings, establishing a shared language for analysis.

Status: Draft

Consumer Housing Choices in Wooden Multi-Story Buildings

A study of residents of non-exposed mass timber multi-story buildings in Finland and Sweden, examining what drove their housing decisions and their experiences.

Status: Published

Supply-Side · Industry Focused

Firm Financial Fitness

Strengthening the financial capacity of mass timber firms to compete for capital, grow, and generate market clearing returns.

Status: Feasibility Underway

Product Cost Stabilization Program

A wood-fiber hedging mechanism, via a manufacturer-owned insurance mutual, enabling mass timber products to bid with construction-cost price stability comparable to steel and concrete.

Status: Theory Paper Draft

Fiber Supply / Forest Circle of Life

A policy initiative recognizing forestry as a cyclical system, exploring how tax treatment of domestically produced CLT could support the full timber value cycle, active forest management, and replanting.

Status: Proposed

Capital Markets · Investment Focused

Industry Structure

The mass timber industry mapped with Porter's Five Forces as a moving ecosystem, with what-if scenarios a reader can adjust to see how bargaining power and capital flows shift.

Status: Phase 1 posted for comment

Resilience Value Adjustment Tool

An underwriting tool that adjusts discount rates for a building's resilience performance and its location-specific risk. Co-led by Dr. Shiling Pei and Noel Johnson.

Listening to Investors
